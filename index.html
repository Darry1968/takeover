Subdomain Takeover Proof of Concept

This page is served from a subdomain whose DNS record pointed to an unclaimed third-party resource. The resource was claimed by a security researcher from eciso.ai to demonstrate that the subdomain can be taken over.

This page is static and harmless: it loads no external scripts, sets no cookies, and collects no data.
